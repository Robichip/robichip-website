Key Takeaways

Why Power Density Matters for Physical AI

為什麼 Physical AI 需要高功率密度動力系統

The talk focused on how UAVs and intelligent machines require a new power platform: compact, thermally aware, measurable, and ready for real system validation.

本次演講聚焦於無人機與智慧機器為何需要新的動力平台：更小型化、具備熱路徑思維、 可量測，並能支援真實系統驗證。

Physical AI Needs Motion Physical AI 需要動力系統

AI moving into the physical world requires reliable, compact, and efficient motion systems.

UAV Propulsion Is System-level 無人機推進是系統問題

ESC current rating alone is not enough. Motor-driver matching, thermal path, weight, and validation matter.

Power SoC Enables Integration Power SoC 支援高整合

RobiSoC aims to integrate power, control, packaging, and thermal path into a compact motion platform.

RobiThrust Validates Propulsion RobiThrust 驗證推進系統

RobiThrust connects RobiDev, motor, propeller, measurement, and thermal observation for UAV validation.

Partners Are Welcome 歡迎國際與產業合作

RobiChip welcomes UAV, robotics, MCU, packaging, system, and overseas partners for pilot collaboration.
