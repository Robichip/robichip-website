INDUSTRY PERSPECTIVE

Evolution of UAV Propulsion Architecture

From Propulsion Gen.1 to Gen.3

無人機推進架構的世代演進：從分離式系統走向半導體定義推進

RobiChip proposes a three-generation framework to describe how UAV propulsion may evolve from discrete components, to integrated modules, and toward semiconductor-defined systems built around power intelligence.

羅比芯提出無人機推進架構的三代演進框架：從 ESC、馬達與螺旋槳 分離的傳統系統，走向基礎整合模組，再進一步邁向以 Power SoC 為核心，整合功率、感測、控制、保護與熱管理的半導體定義推進架構。

PROPULSION GEN.1
DISCRETE

Discrete Architecture

傳統分離式架構

ESC, motor, and propeller are selected, connected, and validated as separate components.

ESC、馬達與螺旋槳分別選型與整合，系統設計主要由個別零組件規格決定。

  • Separate ESC, motor, and propeller
  • Longer electrical and thermal paths
  • Higher wiring and integration effort
Component-defined
→
PROPULSION GEN.2
INTEGRATED MODULE

Integrated Module

基礎整合模組

Drive electronics and motor functions begin to move into a more compact and modular propulsion architecture.

驅動器與馬達開始整合，降低配線與體積，但功率、控制、熱路徑及槳葉 尚未形成完整的跨領域協同設計。

  • Driver and motor integration
  • Reduced system size and wiring
  • Partial electrical and mechanical integration
Module-defined
→
PROPULSION GEN.3
SEMICONDUCTOR-DEFINED

Semiconductor-Defined Propulsion

半導體定義推進架構

Power SoC becomes the intelligence core connecting power, sensing, control, protection, thermal design, motor, and propeller.

以 Power SoC 作為功率智慧核心，整合功率、感測、控制、保護、 熱管理、馬達與槳葉，形成可量測、可驗證與可模組化的推進平台。

  • Power, sensing, control, and protection
  • Motor, propeller, and thermal-path co-design
  • Measurable and repeatable validation
Power-intelligence-defined
THE KEY TRANSITION
UAV propulsion is evolving from component-defined systems toward semiconductor-defined power and motion platforms.

無人機推進系統正從由個別零組件規格主導的架構， 逐步走向由半導體層級的功率智慧、控制、感測與熱管理共同定義的平台。

Validation Workflow

From Bench Test to Customer Pilot

從台架測試走向客戶 pilot

1. Setup Definition motor, propeller, RobiDev, voltage, fixture
2. Static Thrust Test thrust, RPM, input power, current observation
3. Thermal Observation RobiDev T1, motor temperature, housing temperature
4. Product Mapping mini-E / E / P / X / XL positioning
5. Pilot Recommendation next test, design-in path, package quotation
RobiThrust validation does not rely on ESC current rating alone. It uses system-level evidence such as thrust efficiency, motor-driver matching, total system weight, wiring, control behavior, and thermal observation.

RobiThrust 的驗證不只看 ESC 電流規格，而是從推力效率、motor-driver matching、 系統重量、線束、控制行為與熱觀察等系統級證據進行評估。
